# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PCS suppliers, C&I BESS developers, EPCs, system integrators, and investors or strategic partners evaluating Glass Energy Systems as a focused power-electronics deployment partner for India.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Glass Energy Systems as a hard-tech energy infrastructure company building a standardized 100kW bidirectional PCS deployment path for C&I battery storage sites, with a credible roadmap toward larger variants and localized PCS capability.

For exact audience messaging, approved claims, prohibited claims, and current-stage copy, use `BRAND_MESSAGING.md`.

## Brand Personality

Precise, grounded, and restrained. The experience should feel like engineered power infrastructure: calm, technical, physical, and investment-ready.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI startup gradients, SaaS dashboard tropes, random glassmorphism, fake telemetry, decorative card grids, invented certifications or customer proof, over-explained copy, and motion that does not clarify power flow or hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. Show the physical system before abstract claims.
2. Keep technical copy specific, short, and credible.
3. Make battery, PCS, grid, controls, validation, and field support relationships easy to understand.
4. Preserve a premium hard-tech mood through disciplined spacing, restrained color, and purposeful motion.
5. Be honest about current stage, contact behavior, and roadmap maturity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA fundamentals: readable contrast, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, useful alt text, keyboard-accessible navigation and form controls, responsive touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
